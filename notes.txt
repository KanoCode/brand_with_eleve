In Algolia speak, a facet has to do with the attributes/properties of the data that you are filtering.

a facet is a data property that you want to filter by

the key is you have to configure your index to filter those attributes, when you do, then they will appear on the frontend of your site. 

go to manage index, 
go to configuration, 
filtering and faceting

facets -> choose and save the property to facet :)
Facet display -> you can now set that facet to display in the UI, 

then follow the documentation for refinement list to see this facet in action
